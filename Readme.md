**ScanVerve POS** is a Windows billing, inventory, and store management system built specifically for **pharmacies, grocery marts, restaurants, cafes, bakeries, retail stores, and wholesale counters in Pakistan**.

Run fast offline billing with thermal receipt printing on your counter PC, or choose a **Fully Managed Store System** where server management, database backups, updates, and ongoing support are handled for you—so you can focus strictly on your counter.

🔗 **Official Website & Live Demo:** [https://scanverve.online](https://scanverve.online)

---

## Key Capabilities

- **Zero-Downtime Offline Counter Core:** Ring up items in seconds without waiting for a webpage to load—works smoothly even with zero internet connection.
- **Thermal Receipt Printing:** Instant **58mm & 80mm** receipt printing for fast counter checkouts.
- **Phone as a Barcode Scanner *(Version 3)*:** Scan product boxes and medicines directly using your smartphone camera without needing a dedicated USB barcode scanner.
- **Remote Mobile Access *(Version 3)*:** Monitor sales, manage inventory, and run your POS from your phone anywhere, anytime.
- **Staff Security & Daily Protection:** Controlled cashier access with **Staff PIN locks**, clean refund/return audit trails, and **automatic daily backups**.
- **Delivery & Online Ordering Controls:** Integrated ordering website with delivery radius limits, **DPK (per-km charge)**, minimum order rules, and a **1-click ON/OFF** store switch inside the POS.

## Plans & Editions Overview

ScanVerve offers flexible deployment models tailored to Pakistani businesses (all pricing in **PKR**):

### 1. Version 3 — Fully Managed Store System *(Recommended)*
- **Pricing:** Starting from **Rs. 2,000/month** (+ Rs. 3,000 one-time setup) | *14-day free trial with all features unlocked* | *Annual billing: Pay 11 months, get 12*
- **Core Includes:** Standard POS (billing, inventory, dashboard, sales history), phone barcode scanning, remote phone access, managed database/server, automatic updates, free future features, and ongoing technical support.
- **Popular Bundles:**
  - **Mart & Pharmacy Bundle (`Rs. 2,500/mo`):** Standard POS + Loose Weight/Scale Billing + Udhar/Credit Khata.
  - **Restaurant Pro Bundle (`Rs. 3,600/mo`):** Standard POS + KOT & Table Management + Online Ordering Website.
- **Modular Add-Ons:**
  - *Loose Weight / Scale Billing* (`+ Rs. 500/mo`) — Quick buttons for ¼ kg, ½ kg, 1 kg, or custom weight/price calculation.
  - *Udhar / Credit Khata* (`+ Rs. 500/mo`) — Credit limits, partial payments, transparent ledger, and 1-click WhatsApp payment reminders.
  - *KOT & Table Management* (`+ Rs. 1,000/mo`) — Table QR ordering or waiter mobile ordering with instant kitchen tickets.
  - *Online Ordering Website* (`+ Rs. 1,000/mo`) — Custom ordering storefront with delivery radius and per-km pricing.

### 2. Version 1 — Offline POS *(One-Time License)*
